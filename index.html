🔐 Image privacy
Your image is processed in this browser page; TrustLens has no image-upload server. The page downloads its OCR engine and language data from a content-delivery network. Recent history stores result summaries only in this browser; it does not save images or scanned text. Avoid uploading private documents or sensitive personal information.
